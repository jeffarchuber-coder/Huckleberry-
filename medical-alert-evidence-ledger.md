## Editorial Cautions

Product pages must avoid copying the manufacturer’s superlatives or using third-party star ratings. They should instead show a short, sourced feature summary and a plain-language “where it may not fit” section. Fixed prices, promotions, response-time claims, and coverage claims should not be published in evergreen copy because they can change; direct visitors to the official product page to confirm current terms.

The practical safety context is that fall prevention and emergency planning require more than a device. CDC STEADI provides caregiver and patient resources about fall prevention, while NCOA explains that medical-alert systems are products requiring evaluation rather than a replacement for individualized care or emergency planning.
